FIGURE 5.3 EXPLANATION: SHORT-TIME FOURIER TRANSFORM (STFT) SPECTROGRAMS
=============================================================================
Target Image: results/figures/fig_5_3_qneuro_time_frequency_spectrograms.png
Report Chapter: Chapter 4 (Results & Performance Analysis), Section 4.5.2
Spectrogram Parameters: N_perseg = 64, N_overlap = 56, Sampling Rate = 250 Hz, Color Map = Magma (-10 to +35 dB/Hz)

1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This 4-panel time-frequency visualization depicts the transient dynamic evolution of neural and 
muscular rhythms across four operational states: (a) REST baseline, (b) JAW_CLENCH myoelectric burst, 
(c) DOUBLE_BLINK ocular deflection, and (d) HAND_CLENCH sensorimotor rhythm modulation.

2. PANEL DETAILS & DYNAMIC TIME-FREQUENCY PROFILES
--------------------------------------------------
Top-Left: REST (Baseline State, Channel 1)
- Shows quiescent background electrophysiology across the 3.0-second epoch.
- Consistent low-power background (< 5 dB/Hz) across 0–50 Hz, with a narrow stationary alpha 
  rhythm track at ~10 Hz representing wakeful idling.

Top-Right: JAW_CLENCH (Temporalis High-Gamma EMG Spike, Channel 1)
- Temporal Evolution: Saccadic onset at t = 0.5 s lasting through t = 2.5 s.
- Spectral Profile: Intense broadband power burst reaching +32 to +35 dB/Hz spanning 25 to 45 Hz.
- Shaded Cyan Band (30–45 Hz): Highlights the target feature extraction band. Energy within this 
  band increases by >25 dB during isometric molar contraction, providing unambiguous trigger gating.

Bottom-Left: DOUBLE_BLINK (Frontal Low-Frequency EOG Deflection, Channel 1)
- Temporal Evolution: Two discrete high-energy impulses centered at t = 0.8 s and t = 1.3 s (Δt = 500 ms).
- Spectral Profile: Energy is strictly concentrated below 6 Hz (>20 dB/Hz), decaying rapidly to 
  baseline above 10 Hz. High-gamma power remains completely undisturbed (< 0 dB/Hz), demonstrating 
  the absence of false EMG trigger leakage during blinks.

Bottom-Right: HAND_CLENCH (Sensorimotor Mu/Beta Desynchronization, Channel 3)
- Temporal Evolution: Sustained 3.0-second overt motor execution.
- Spectral Profile: Shaded lime band (8–13 Hz) illustrates significant post-movement attenuation 
  (Event-Related Desynchronization, ERD). The resting alpha/mu rhythm drops by 4–6 dB as pyramidal 
  motor neurons become active.

3. CLINICAL & CONTROL IMPLICATIONS
----------------------------------
- High Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR): The jaw-clench EMG burst exhibits an SNR > 20 dB over resting 
  background, enabling rapid detection within a 200 ms sliding window.
- Low-Latency Preemption: Spectrogram dynamics show that the onset latency of both blinks and 
  clenches is under 50 ms from physical initiation, well within the clinical safety threshold (< 100 ms).
