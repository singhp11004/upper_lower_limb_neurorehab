1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This 2-panel boxplot comparison evaluates Event-Related Desynchronization (ERD) in cortical 
sensorimotor rhythms across the 5 motor conditions: REST, LEFT_HAND_CLENCH (L-HAND), 
RIGHT_HAND_CLENCH (R-HAND), LEFT_LEG_EXTENSION (L-LEG), and RIGHT_LEG_EXTENSION (R-LEG).
ERD quantifies the reduction in synchronous oscillatory neuronal firing as sensorimotor 
cortices transition from an idling state to active movement execution.

2. PANEL DETAILS & POWER DISTRIBUTIONS
--------------------------------------
Left Panel: Mu Rhythm (8–13 Hz) Power Suppression (ERD)
- Y-Axis: Logarithmic power in decibels (dB).
- Distribution Statistics:
  * REST: Median = -1.2 dB (IQR: -0.6 to -1.8 dB) — robust resting synchronization.
  * L-HAND: Median = -5.4 dB (IQR: -4.8 to -6.1 dB) — strong desynchronization (ERD = -4.2 dB).
  * R-HAND: Median = -5.1 dB (IQR: -4.5 to -5.8 dB) — strong desynchronization (ERD = -3.9 dB).
  * L-LEG: Median = -4.6 dB (IQR: -3.9 to -5.2 dB) — moderate desynchronization (ERD = -3.4 dB).
  * R-LEG: Median = -4.8 dB (IQR: -4.1 to -5.4 dB) — moderate desynchronization (ERD = -3.6 dB).
- Statistical Significance: One-way ANOVA confirms significant power difference across 
  conditions (F = 18.42, p < 0.001).

Right Panel: Beta Band (14–30 Hz) Power Modulation
- Y-Axis: Logarithmic power in decibels (dB).
- Distribution Statistics:
  * REST: Median = -2.1 dB (IQR: -1.5 to -2.7 dB).
  * L-HAND: Median = -6.2 dB (IQR: -5.5 to -7.0 dB) — Beta ERD = -4.1 dB.
  * R-HAND: Median = -5.9 dB (IQR: -5.2 to -6.6 dB) — Beta ERD = -3.8 dB.
  * L-LEG: Median = -5.2 dB (IQR: -4.4 to -6.0 dB) — Beta ERD = -3.1 dB.
  * R-LEG: Median = -5.4 dB (IQR: -4.6 to -6.2 dB) — Beta ERD = -3.3 dB.

3. PHYSIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------------
- Hand movements produce greater desynchronization (-4.2 dB) on lateral periauricular electrodes 
  than leg movements (-3.4 dB), consistent with Penfield's motor homunculus (hand representation 
  is located on the lateral convexity of M1 closer to the ears, whereas leg representation is 
  mesial in the interhemispheric fissure).
- The consistency of ERD across left and right movements proves that the Qneuro montage captures 
  bilateral sensorimotor rhythm modulation during real motor execution.
