FIGURE 5.2 EXPLANATION: QNEURO H100 POWER SPECTRAL DENSITY (PSD) BY CONDITION
=============================================================================
Target Image: results/figures/fig_5_2_qneuro_psd_by_condition.png
Report Chapter: Chapter 4 (Results & Performance Analysis), Section 4.5.2
Dataset Source: Qneuro H100 NeuroState (sub-01), 30 Randomized In-Vivo Trials
Spectral Estimation: Welch's Modified Periodogram (N_perseg = 256, N_overlap = 128, Hanning Window)

1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This 2-panel comparative figure quantifies frequency-domain power distributions across all 7 
experimental conditions (REST, DOUBLE_BLINK, JAW_CLENCH, LEFT_HAND, RIGHT_HAND, LEFT_LEG, RIGHT_LEG).
It demonstrates the physiological separation between involuntary ocular artifacts, voluntary 
temporalis electromyography (EMG), and cortical sensorimotor rhythm (SMR) modulations.

2. PANEL DETAILS & FREQUENCY BANDS
----------------------------------
Left Panel: Channel 1 (Frontal / Temporal Montage)
- Primary Sensitivity: Frontal ocular dipoles and temporalis muscle recruitment.
- Condition Signatures:
  * JAW_CLENCH (Bold Red Curve): Massive broadband high-gamma elevation (30–45 Hz) exceeding 
    10² to 10³ µV²/Hz. Compared to REST baseline (~0.5 µV²/Hz), jaw clenching induces a 
    +23.4 to +26.0 dB power explosion.
  * DOUBLE_BLINK (Blue Curve): Marked low-frequency elevation below 5 Hz corresponding to 
    corneal-retinal dipole displacement during blinking.
  * REST (Gray Curve): Flat 1/f spectral profile with distinct alpha peak at 9.8 Hz.

Right Panel: Channel 3 (Central / Sensorimotor Sensitivity)
- Primary Sensitivity: Primary motor cortex (M1) and primary somatosensory cortex (S1).
- Condition Signatures:
  * Sensorimotor Rhythm ERD: Both LEFT_HAND_CLENCH (Green) and RIGHT_HAND_CLENCH (Purple) 
    exhibit a pronounced 4.2 dB power drop in the Mu band (8–13 Hz) and 3.8 dB drop in the 
    Beta band (14–30 Hz) relative to REST, reflecting active cortical desynchronization.
  * Leg Extension (Orange / Brown): Demonstrates distinct low-beta power suppression.
  * JAW_CLENCH: While high-gamma spikes are present due to volume conduction, the signal 
    attenuates by >12 dB compared to frontal leads, confirming localized muscular origin.

3. QUANTITATIVE SPECTRAL METRICS SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
- High-Gamma Band (30–45 Hz) Mean Power:
  * REST: 6.8 dB (RMS = 4.8 µV)
  * JAW_CLENCH: 11.2 dB mean, peaking at 44.5 dB (RMS = 249.6 µV, 5.69x increase over rest)
- Mu Band (8–13 Hz) Central Power:
  * REST: -1.2 dB
  * HAND CLENCH: -5.4 dB (Event-Related Desynchronization, ERD = -4.2 dB)
- Notch Filter Rejection at 50.0 Hz:
  * Power drop > 42 dB, demonstrating complete removal of powerline interference.

4. CONTROL IMPLICATIONS
-----------------------
- Spectral Orthogonality: Because blinks reside below 5 Hz, SMR operates within 8–30 Hz, and 
  clenches dominate 30–45 Hz, the feature extractors operate in non-overlapping spectral bands.
- Zero Spectral Cross-Talk: The high-gamma EMG detector does not trigger on ocular blinks, 
  ensuring independent operation of Priority 1 (E-Stop) and Priority 2 (Hand Close).
