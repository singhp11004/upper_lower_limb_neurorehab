1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This 5x5 confusion matrix evaluates the system's ability to decode movement laterality (left vs. 
right side) in addition to body part (hand vs. leg) on real participant data. This capability is 
fundamental for hemiparetic rehabilitation where targeted therapy must isolate the impaired limb.

4. LATERALITY RESOLUTION ANALYSIS
---------------------------------
- Ipsilateral vs. Contralateral Confusion: Misclassifying Left Hand as Right Hand occurred in 
  only 5.7% of trials, and Right Hand as Left Hand occurred in 8.8% of trials.
- Homunculus Dominance: Hand lateralization is sharper than leg lateralization, attributable to 
  the greater cortical magnification factor and wider interhemispheric dipole distance of hand areas.
