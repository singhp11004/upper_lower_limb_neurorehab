1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This horizontal bar chart identifies which electrophysiological features and sensorimotor 
channels contribute the greatest discriminative weight in decoding movement intent (Rest vs. 
Hand vs. Leg). It validates the neurophysiological hypothesis that central sensorimotor rhythms 
(Mu and Beta) provide the primary neural substrates for voluntary motor intention.

3. NEUROBIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
---------------------------------
- The dominant loading on Channels 3 and 4 confirms that the classifier is leveraging genuine 
  sensorimotor rhythms over motor strip somatotopies, rather than relying on peripheral 
  muscular artifacts or frontal eye movements.
- High-gamma features on Channel 5 contribute modestly (~5.8%) to movement classification, 
  ensuring that the motor intent classifier remains decoupled from the high-gamma jaw-clench detector.
