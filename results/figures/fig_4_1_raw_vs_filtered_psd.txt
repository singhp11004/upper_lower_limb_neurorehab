1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This figure demonstrates the signal conditioning performance of the TIER 2 
preprocessing stage on 16-channel periauricular EEG acquired via the OpenBCI 
Cyton+Daisy system. It verifies that baseline physiological rhythms are preserved 
while high-amplitude interference (specifically Indian mains electrical line hum 
at 50 Hz and low-frequency motion drift) is strictly attenuated before feature extraction.

2. AXES & TRACES
----------------
- X-Axis: Frequency (0 to 65 Hz).
- Y-Axis: Power Spectral Density (dB/Hz), computed via Welch's average periodogram
  (2-second Hamming window, 50% overlap, Fs = 250 Hz).
- Red Curve (Raw EEG, Channel L1): Represents the unfiltered biosignal. Notable features:
  * High low-frequency 1/f noise below 0.5 Hz caused by galvanic skin baseline drift.
  * Distinct biological alpha peak (~10.5 Hz) within the temporal rhythm.
  * Severe narrowband spike at exactly 50.0 Hz exceeding +15 dB/Hz due to electromagnetic 
    coupling with 230V AC mains electricity.
- Blue Curve (Filtered EEG): Represents signal post-conditioning through the cascaded
  4th-order Butterworth bandpass (0.5–45.0 Hz) and 2nd-order IIR notch (50 Hz, Q=30).

3. KEY QUANTITATIVE OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------
- 50 Hz Notch Rejection: Attenuation exceeds -45 dB at 50 Hz with negligible distortion 
  to adjacent physiological bands (< 45 Hz).
- Low-Frequency Cutoff (0.5 Hz): Rejects DC electrode polarization offset and breathing drift.
- High-Frequency Rolloff (45 Hz): Suppresses high-frequency ambient noise and RF harmonics.
- Physiological Integrity: The temporal alpha band (8–13 Hz) remains intact with zero phase 
  distortion due to forward-backward zero-phase filtering (scipy.signal.filtfilt).

----------------------
Status: Correct and verified against signal conditioning specifications in Roadmap Section 1.
