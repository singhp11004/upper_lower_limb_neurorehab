FIGURE 4.2 EXPLANATION: P300 ERP GRAND AVERAGE WAVEFORM
=============================================================================
Target Image: results/figures/fig_4_2_p300_erp_grand_average.png
Report Chapter: Chapter 4 (Results & Performance Analysis), Section 4.2

1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This figure plots the time-locked Event-Related Potential (ERP) elicited during 
the oddball paradigm (auditory/tactile stimulus), comparing the neuronal response 
to rare target stimuli versus frequent non-target stimuli across periauricular 
cEEGrid channels (specifically mid-temporal leads L3/R3). It validates the biological 
feasibility of extracting P300 potentials without scalp-vertex electrodes.

2. AXES & TRACES
----------------
- X-Axis: Time relative to stimulus onset (milliseconds), spanning from -200 ms 
  (pre-stimulus baseline) to +800 ms post-stimulus.
- Y-Axis: Microvolt amplitude (µV).
- Red Solid Curve (Target Stimulus): The grand-averaged waveform across target trials.
  * Shows a pronounced positive deflection peaking at approximately +4.5 µV around 
    350 ms post-stimulus (characteristic P3b latency on periauricular montage).
- Grey Dashed Curve (Non-Target Stimulus): The grand-averaged waveform across frequent 
  distractor stimuli, showing baseline fluctuation around 0 µV without positive deflection.
- Blue Shaded Region (250–500 ms): The time-locked feature extraction epoch used by 
  P300Extractor. This window captures the full morphology of the P300 crest.

3. KEY QUANTITATIVE OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------
- Signal-to-Noise Separation: Peak target amplitude (+4.5 µV) is over 4.5× the non-target 
  standard deviation (approx 0.8 µV), providing the statistical margin required for LDA separation.
- Downsampling & Feature Dimensionality: The 250–500 ms window (63 samples at 250 Hz) 
  is decimate-downsampled by a factor of 4 to yield 16 discrete time-points per channel.
  Across 16 channels, this forms a 256-dimensional feature vector before SWLDA selection.

4. VERIFICATION STATUS
----------------------
Status: Correct and verified against literature benchmarks (Debener et al. 2015, Bleichner et al. 2016).
