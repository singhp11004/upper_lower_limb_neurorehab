1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This figure presents the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) time-frequency representation 
of a deliberate jaw-clench event captured across anterior temporalis-adjacent electrodes 
on the cEEGrid array. It proves that temporalis muscle recruitment produces an abrupt, 
high-energy broadband spectral burst strictly localized to the 30–45 Hz high-gamma sub-band, 
serving as a robust biometric trigger for hand closure (0x20) or leg stepping (0x30).

2. SPECTROGRAM PARAMETERS & AXES
--------------------------------
- X-Axis: Time in seconds (0.0 to 4.0 s).
- Y-Axis: Frequency in Hertz (0 to 60 Hz).
- Color Intensity: Power spectral density in dB/Hz, ranging from quiet resting baseline (-20 dB) 
  to saturated clench energy (+25 dB).
- Windowing: Short-Time Fourier Transform with N_perseg = 64 points (256 ms), N_overlap = 48 points 
  (75% overlap) at Fs = 250 Hz.
- White Bounding Box (t = 1.5 s to 2.5 s): Deliberate isometric jaw-clench interval.
- Red Horizontal Dashed Lines: High-gamma bandpass boundaries (30.0 Hz to 45.0 Hz).

3. KEY QUANTITATIVE OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------
- High-Gamma Power Elevation: During resting baseline (t < 1.5 s), high-gamma energy remains 
  under -2 dB. Upon jaw clench onset (t = 1.5 s), power surges by +18 to +24 dB across the 30–45 Hz band.
- Low-Frequency Decoupling: Unlike eye blinks which manifest below 10 Hz, the jaw-clench EMG signature 
  is concentrated above 25 Hz, permitting clean frequency-domain separation without interference.
- Trigger Generation: JawClenchDetector tracks high-gamma envelope elevation; when sustained above 
  the threshold for > 200 ms and validated by the 300 ms dwell timer, Hand Close command 0x20 is dispatched.
