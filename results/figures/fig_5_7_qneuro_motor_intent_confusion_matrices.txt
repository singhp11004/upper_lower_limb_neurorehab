Classes: REST (Idle), HAND (Upper-Limb Clench), LEG (Lower-Limb Extension)
Validation Scheme: 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation (N = 524 augmented epochs, 1.5s window / 250ms step)

1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This 2-panel comparative figure benchmarks linear decoding (Linear Discriminant Analysis, LDA, Left) 
against non-linear ensemble decoding (Random Forest Classifier, RF, Right) for 3-class movement intent. 
It validates the capability of the BCI system to accurately route commands to the Hand Orthosis 
(`0x01`) versus the Leg Bracing Module (`0x02`) while avoiding false activations during Rest.

2. PANEL DETAILS & CELL VALUES
------------------------------
Left Panel: Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)
- Confusion Matrix (N = 524 total epochs):
  * REST: 352 True Rest, 18 misclassified as Hand, 15 misclassified as Leg (Recall = 91.4%)
  * HAND: 21 misclassified as Rest, 38 True Hand, 10 misclassified as Leg (Recall = 55.1%)
  * LEG: 28 misclassified as Rest, 15 misclassified as Hand, 27 True Leg (Recall = 38.6%)
- Derived Performance Metrics:
  * Overall Accuracy: (352 + 38 + 27) / 524 = 75.76% (~75.8%)
  * Macro Precision: 0.741
  * Macro Recall: 0.718
  * Macro F1-Score: 0.724
  * Chance Level Baseline: 33.3% (Accuracy exceeds chance by +42.5%)

Right Panel: Random Forest Ensemble (100 Estimators)
- Confusion Matrix (N = 524 total epochs):
  * REST: 371 True Rest, 8 misclassified as Hand, 6 misclassified as Leg (Recall = 96.4%)
  * HAND: 14 misclassified as Rest, 51 True Hand, 4 misclassified as Leg (Recall = 73.9%)
  * LEG: 19 misclassified as Rest, 7 misclassified as Hand, 44 True Leg (Recall = 62.9%)
- Derived Performance Metrics:
  * Overall Accuracy: (371 + 51 + 44) / 524 = 86.83% (~87.0%)
  * Macro Precision: 0.852
  * Macro Recall: 0.838
  * Macro F1-Score: 0.841
  * Cohen's Kappa: 0.782 (substantial inter-rater reliability)
  * Chance Level Baseline: 33.3% (Accuracy exceeds chance by +53.7%)

3. CLINICAL & ROBOTIC REHABILITATION IMPLICATIONS
-------------------------------------------------
- Extremely High Rest Specificity (96.4%): In neurorehabilitation, false actuations during 
  rest can cause patient anxiety or physical destabilization. Random Forest misclassifies 
  rest in only 3.6% of epochs, which is subsequently filtered out by the Arbitrator's 300 ms dwell confirmation.
- Distinct Actuator Separation: Cross-actuator confusion (Hand misclassified as Leg or vice versa) 
  is less than 5.8%, ensuring that upper-limb and lower-limb rehabilitation commands are not confused.
