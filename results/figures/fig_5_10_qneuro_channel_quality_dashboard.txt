1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This 4-panel diagnostic dashboard provides the empirical justification for channel selection 
and automated bad-lead rejection. Wearable dry and semi-dry BCI montages frequently encounter 
variable skin contact impedance across different head geometries. The dashboard evaluates DC 
bias, RMS noise floor, and dynamic range, demonstrating how the Signal Quality Monitor (SQM) 
automatically isolates Channels 1–5 and rejects Channels 6–8.
