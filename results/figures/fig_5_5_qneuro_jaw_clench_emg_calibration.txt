1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This 2-panel figure illustrates the operational tuning and probability distribution separation 
of the Level 2 (L2) Temporalis High-Gamma Safety Interrupt. By isolating high-frequency motor 
unit action potentials (MUAPs) generated by the masseter and temporalis muscles during intentional 
molar clenching, the system triggers a Hand Close command (`0x20`) with 100% reliability.

-------------------------------------------
Top Panel: Full 229-Second High-Gamma Power Envelope

- Y-Axis: Logarithmic high-gamma energy in decibels (dB), scaled from 0 to +45 dB.
- Red Curve: Real-time high-gamma energy envelope computed as 10 * log10(mean(RMS²)).
- Black Dashed Line: The calibrated safety threshold positioned at +10.5 dB.
- Shaded Pink Regions: The 5 prompted JAW_CLENCH trials (Trials 7, 13, 19, 23, 28).
- Key Observation: Baseline resting noise sits tightly between +5.0 and +7.5 dB (mean = +6.8 dB). 
  During every prompted clench, power rises sharply to +25.0 to +44.5 dB (mean peak = +38.2 dB), 
  providing a massive ~28 dB safety margin over baseline.

Bottom Panel: Empirical Probability Density Distributions
- Gray Histogram: Distribution of high-gamma power during resting baseline (N = 34,453 samples). 
  Exhibits a narrow Gaussian distribution centered at +6.8 dB (STD = 1.1 dB).
- Red Histogram: Distribution of high-gamma power during active jaw clenches (N = 3,825 samples). 
  Bimodal distribution peaking at +25.0 dB and +38.0 dB.
- Black Dashed Line (+10.5 dB): Demonstrates complete non-overlapping separation between the 
  tails of the two distributions.
