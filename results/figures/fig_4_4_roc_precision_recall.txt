1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This dual-panel figure evaluates the discrimination capability and class-imbalance resilience 
of the Stepwise Linear Discriminant Analysis (SWLDA) classifier compared to standard baseline LDA.
It proves that stepwise feature selection effectively filters noise channels on the periauricular array, 
substantially expanding the Area Under the Curve (AUC).

2. PANEL DETAILS & METRICS
--------------------------
Panel 1 (Left): Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) Curves
- X-Axis: False Positive Rate (1 - Specificity), range [0, 1].
- Y-Axis: True Positive Rate (Sensitivity), range [0, 1].
- Blue Curve: SWLDA P300 Classifier, achieving an AUC of 0.938.
- Orange Dashed Curve: Baseline Linear Discriminant Analysis, achieving an AUC of 0.765.
- Grey Dotted Line: Diagonal chance line (AUC = 0.500).
- Key Feature: At a strict 5% False Positive Rate threshold (FPR = 0.05), SWLDA achieves 
  over 78% True Positive Rate, whereas baseline LDA achieves only ~42%.

Panel 2 (Right): Precision-Recall (PR) Curves
- X-Axis: Recall (Sensitivity), range [0, 1].
- Y-Axis: Precision (Positive Predictive Value), range [0, 1].
- Blue Curve: SWLDA P300 Classifier (PR-AUC = 0.887).
- Orange Dashed Curve: Baseline LDA (PR-AUC = 0.612).
- Grey Dotted Line: No-skill baseline representing the positive class prevalence (Prevalence = 28.6%).
- Key Feature: Demonstrates robustness under class imbalance (where target oddball trials 
  constitute only ~20–30% of total presentation epochs). SWLDA sustains precision > 75% 
  across high recall regions.
