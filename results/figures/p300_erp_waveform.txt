LEGACY FIGURE EXPLANATION: P300 EVENT-RELATED POTENTIAL (ERP) AVERAGED WAVEFORM
================================================================================
Target Image: results/figures/p300_erp_waveform.png
Historical Context: Phase 2 Initial Prototype Waveform (Commit d028191)

1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & SIGNIFICANCE
--------------------------------------
This plot displays the original grand-averaged Event-Related Potential (ERP) 
extracted from the 16-channel periauricular array during initial prototype testing. 
It confirms the characteristic latency and morphology of the P300 crest in response 
to oddball target stimuli compared to baseline distractor trials.

2. AXES & TRACES
----------------
- X-Axis: Time Post-Stimulus (milliseconds), ranging from 0 ms to 500 ms.
- Y-Axis: Amplitude potential (arbitrary normalized scale / microvolts), spanning from -0.75 to +0.75.
- Traces:
  * Blue Curve: Target Stimulus (P300 ERP Waveform). Exhibits a sharp positive crest peaking at ~300 ms.
  * Grey Curve: Non-Target Baseline. Fluctuates near 0 with low variance, showing absence of ERP activation.
  * Shaded Region (250–500 ms): Denotes the SWLDA feature extraction window where time-locked amplitude 
    bins are downsampled to form the classification vector.
  * Peak Annotation: Highlights the prominent P300 crest occurring between 280 ms and 320 ms.

3. COMPARISON WITH EXPANDED REPORT FIGURE (FIG 4.2)
---------------------------------------------------
- In this initial prototype figure, the time axis was restricted to post-stimulus only (0 to 500 ms).
- In the updated publication-grade version (`fig_4_2_p300_erp_grand_average.png`), the time baseline was 
  expanded to include pre-stimulus baseline interval (-200 ms to +800 ms), with calibrated microvolt units 
  (peaking at +4.5 µV) and standard academic typography conforming to the IEEE/BCE497J report guidelines.
